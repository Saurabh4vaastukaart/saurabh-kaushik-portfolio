About

A product manager who stays close to the problem.

I enjoy turning an unclear customer or business challenge into a product direction that teams can understand, build and measure.

Based in Gurugram, India Working across AI/LLM, B2B SaaS, healthcare, EdTech and consumer product environments.

My approach

Clarity before velocity.

Fast execution matters, but only after the team understands the user problem, the trade-offs and what success should look like.

I have spent six years owning products and customer journeys across multiple business models. My work covers product discovery, strategy, roadmapping, prioritisation, PRDs, delivery, go-to-market and post-launch optimisation.

I work closely with founders, designers, engineers, business teams and operations. I am comfortable moving between a customer conversation, a funnel analysis, a technical workflow and an executive decision because each gives a different part of the product picture.

What matters most to me is meaningful ownership: understanding why a problem matters, helping the team choose the right solution, and staying accountable for the result after launch.

Product principles

How I make decisions.

Understand before building

I use customer interviews, behaviour and data to make the problem specific enough for the team to solve.

Keep the MVP honest

An MVP should test the most important assumption while still delivering a coherent experience for the user.

Make ownership end-to-end

Product work continues through adoption, measurement and iteration. Launch is a milestone, not the finish line.
