Case study 04 · Consumer marketplace

Akashvaani

A question-led astrology consulting marketplace designed to make expert discovery, booking and delivery feel clearer and more trustworthy.

In this story The problem My role Product strategy Customer journey Measurement What I learned

01 · The problem

Customers wanted guidance, but trust and clarity were weak.

People looking for astrology consulting often had to choose between many loosely defined services and experts without knowing what they would receive. That uncertainty made both the purchase decision and the final experience harder.

The product needed to help a customer express a real concern, choose the right expert and understand what would happen after payment.

02 · My role

Shape the proposition and rebuild the experience.

I owned the product revamp across problem framing, roadmap, expert discovery, booking and customer experience. I worked with design and engineering as the platform moved from a WordPress/PHP foundation to Next.js and Node.js.

My focus was not simply modernising the technology. It was using the rebuild to make the service easier to understand and more consistent from question to delivered consultation.

03 · Product strategy

Reduce breadth to increase confidence.

01

Lead with one high-quality service

Instead of presenting a long catalogue, the experience focused attention on a clear consulting outcome.

02

Organise around real life questions

Health, career, relationships and marriage became the main categories because they matched the concerns customers brought to the platform.

03

Balance guidance with flexibility

A curated question bank helped customers who did not know how to phrase a concern, while custom questions remained available.

04 · Customer journey

Make the service feel concrete before purchase.

Choose a concernStart with a meaningful life category.
Frame the questionSelect a curated prompt or write a custom one.
Book an expertMove through a clearer discovery and payment journey.
Receive guidanceComplete the journey with delivered consultancy.

Consultancy delivery became the North Star because it represented the completed value exchange: the customer received the guidance they had purchased, not merely a booked order.

05 · Measurement

Track whether clearer value improves monetisation.

Average revenue per user increased from ₹420 to ₹610, a 45% improvement. The metric reflected whether customers saw enough value and clarity in the service to complete higher-value transactions.

06 · What I learned

More choice can reduce confidence.

In a trust-sensitive marketplace, a focused proposition can be more valuable than a large catalogue. Clear categories, good question framing and a predictable delivery journey helped customers understand what they were buying.
