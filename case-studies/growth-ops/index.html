Case study 02 · Marketing intelligence

Growth Ops

An AI/LLM acquisition and attribution product built to help marketing teams connect spend, conversion and business performance more clearly.

Product
B2B SaaS
Domain
Marketing intelligence

Product Manager

Primary outcome

ROAS increased from 2.9x to 4.1x.

Conversion also increased from 2.8% to 3.6%, linking product performance to commercial outcomes rather than activity alone.

In this story The problem My role Product approach Measurement What I learned

01 · The problem

Acquisition data only matters when it improves a decision.

Marketing teams need to understand which acquisition activity is turning into valuable customer behaviour. The product opportunity was to make acquisition and attribution signals easier to interpret and more useful for ongoing optimisation.

The commercial test was clear: could the product help improve conversion and generate more return from the same area of spend?

02 · My role

Ownership from product definition through adoption.

I led the AI/LLM product from definition and prioritisation through cross-functional execution, launch readiness and post-launch optimisation.

The work required aligning the product experience with the outcomes acquisition teams already cared about, while keeping business stakeholders and the delivery team focused on a shared measure of success.

03 · Product approach

Build around the decision loop, not a collection of dashboards.

01

Anchor the product in commercial metrics

Conversion and return on ad spend were treated as outcome measures, keeping prioritisation connected to customer and business value.

02

Connect acquisition with attribution

The product story linked incoming demand with downstream results so teams could judge performance as one journey.

03

Plan for post-launch optimisation

Launch readiness, adoption and iteration were part of the roadmap rather than separate activities after engineering delivery.

Confidentiality noteThis case study focuses on the product objective and published outcome metrics. Customer-specific data and implementation details are intentionally excluded.

04 · Measurement

Use two metrics to see efficiency and effectiveness together.

Conversion showed whether more traffic moved into the intended outcome. It improved from 2.8% to 3.6%, a relative increase of 29%.

ROAS showed whether acquisition became more commercially efficient. It improved from 2.9x to 4.1x, a relative increase of 41%.

2.8% → 3.6%Conversion rate
2.9x → 4.1xReturn on ad spend

05 · What I learned

An analytics product should shorten the path from signal to action.

The product becomes valuable when teams can use its output to make a better allocation or optimisation decision. That is why the strongest success measures sat outside the interface: conversion and return on spend.
